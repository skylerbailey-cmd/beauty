      Maverick Payments Connection

      Connect this company's Maverick account to reconcile batches against recorded sales (Transactions → Run Batch Audit). The token is stored securely on the server and never shown again.

      Pay Schedule

      When payday falls and which period it settles. Changing this re-dates every paycheck in Reports → Payroll.

      Payarc Connection

      Connect this company's Payarc account. In the Payarc dashboard go to API, click the eye icon on your token's row, and copy the API Bearer Token from the pop-up — not the ID shown in the table, which won't authenticate. Stored on the server and never shown again.

      Don't see your processor? Reconciliation works with Maverick and Payarc today. If your merchant account is with someone else, email support@sky-sale.com and tell us who you process with — we'll look at adding them. Everything else in the register works either way; only Run Batch Audit needs the connection.

      Automatic emails after a sale

      Off, every email after a sale waits for someone to press Send — as it always has. On, they go out by themselves, from your connected Gmail, within a minute of the sale (or after the wait you set).

      Products that don't send

      A sale made up only of these products isn't emailed automatically — gift cards, a service, a sample. Bought alongside anything else, the email still goes.
